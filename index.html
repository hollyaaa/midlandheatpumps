Why Service Your Heat Pump?

A heat pump is one of the largest investments in your home and, like any heating system, it requires regular maintenance to continue operating efficiently and reliably. Annual servicing can help:

  • Maintain system efficiency
  • Reduce the risk of unexpected breakdowns
  • Support manufacturer warranty requirements
  • Identify potential issues before they become costly repairs
  • Extend equipment lifespan
